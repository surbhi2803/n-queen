What is N-Queens?

Place N queens on an N×N chessboard so that no two queens attack each other — not on the same row, column, or diagonal.

How Backtracking Works
1 Place a queen in the first safe column of the current row
2 Move to the next row and repeat
3 If no safe column exists, backtrack to previous row
4 Try the next column in that row and continue
5 Repeat until all queens are placed safely
